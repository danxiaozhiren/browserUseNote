# Product Brief

## Problem

AI news moves too fast. Manually checking official blogs, GitHub, Hugging Face, papers, rankings, and communities every day is time-consuming and noisy.

## Target User

Me, as someone learning Browser Use and tracking AI agent development.

## Job To Be Done

When I want to understand what changed in AI today, I want a concise briefing that filters noise, preserves source links, and explains why each item matters.

## Non-Goals

- Do not generate a long news digest.
- Do not summarize every source.
- Do not treat second-hand media as equal to official sources.
- Do not browse pages when a stable feed or API is enough.

## Success Criteria

- Daily output can be read in less than 10 minutes.
- Each important item includes source link, reason, and relevance.
- The system explicitly lists low-value noisy items.
- Browser automation is used only where it adds value.
